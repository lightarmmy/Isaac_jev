Isaac combat: choose independent move9 and shoot5 controls. Player is the origin;
x points right, y down; positions are world pixels, velocity is the observed game
velocity. Room mask is native 40-pixel cells with a player-relative map origin.
Walls/voids in large and L rooms are blocked. Flying changes pit/ground hazard
traversal but is not immunity to all damage. Direction clearances are approximate
player-sized geometry, not a future simulation. Maintain escape routes and firing
alignment; consider all threat directions, not just the nearest enemy.
Owned item summaries explain effects; actual stats and observed weapon type/charge
take precedence over expected effects. Follow the active weapon trigger: charge_release
holds with a shooting direction and releases on none; charge_auto can fire at full
charge; continuous weapons fire while held. Unknown charge is not zero charge.
Familiars may attack independently. Friendly attacks are not enemy projectiles.
Avoid harmful creep, hostile lasers, imminent bombs, fire and spikes as applicable.
Unknown effects/estimated extents are explicitly marked and need cautious handling.
History contains past physical state only, never future outcomes or previous movement
labels. Positive requests survival with useful fire, negative is a retrospective
damage-associated condition, unknown is unassessed. Active/bomb/card controls are
disabled. Do not infer undiscovered rooms, seeds or unobserved charge values.
Repeated records use fields/rows column tables. Null remains unknown. Text numbers
are rounded to 0.01; numeric features retain precision. Room mask rows use the
legend symbols; local occupancy rows use 0=free and 1=blocked.
